Hi, I'm Lisa, and I'm a little bit wildly wired myself

I feel things deeply. I think in fireworks. I have a million ideas before breakfast and occasionally forget to eat breakfast.

For a long time I wondered why the things that seemed easy for everyone else could feel so bloody hard for me. Understanding my ADHD changed that. The intensity, the overwhelm, the creativity and the big feelings had a name. They weren't flaws. They were wiring.

The hardest bit wasn't the forgotten appointments or the 47 open tabs. It was friendship. People misread you. You go quiet and they think you don't care. You get excited and they think it's too much. I've lost friendships along the way, and that hurt.

What I really wanted was people who get it. So I'm building the thing I wish I'd found: a brand, a community and a creative corner that brings people together, breaks stigma and reminds us that different doesn't mean broken.

For the overthinkers, the oversharers, the big feelers, the late-diagnosed and the still-figuring-it-out crew.

I'm not doing this because I've got all the answers. I'm doing it because I know how much it matters to feel understood.

Pull up a chair. Leave the masking at the door. 💖
